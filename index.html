Welcome to PL Fan Warzone

A free prediction league for the group — predict every gameweek, call the final table, and see who actually knows football.

How it works

🎯
Predict Gameweek — enter a scoreline for every match before kickoff. Once a match starts, that prediction locks.
📋
Predict League Table — drag (or use the ▲▼ buttons on mobile) to set your predicted final standings, any time until the admin locks it.
🗂️
Table — compare everyone's table predictions side by side against the real current standings.
🗣️
Games — see everyone's scoreline predictions for every gameweek, with crowd sentiment and results.
🏆
Leaderboard — everyone's total points, updated automatically about once an hour.
🎖️
Season Awards — bragging-rights picks for Golden Boot, Golden Glove, and more.
👤
My Profile — your full prediction history, accuracy, and current streak.

Scoring

25 pts Exact scoreline correct
10 pts Correct outcome (win/draw/loss), wrong score
+5 / +10 / +20 Streak bonus at 3, 5, or 10 gameweeks running with at least one correct outcome (highest tier only, not stacked)
+15 pts each Correct "highest scoring team" or "clean sheet team" gameweek extra guess
Up to 20 pts/team League table prediction — max(0, 20 − 2×|predicted position − actual position|) per team, checked at gameweek 19 (worth 40%) and again at season end (worth 60%)
Honour system Player-level picks (top scorer, Golden Boot, etc.) aren't auto-scored — the free data feed doesn't include individual player stats

A known issue, now fixed

Some players reported the Predict League Table area taking 5-10 seconds to load or appearing blank. That was a real bug — the page was making one slow network request per fixture, one after another, instead of one combined request. It's fixed now; if it's still slow for you, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R).
