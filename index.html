Welcome to PL Fan Warzone

A free prediction league for the group — predict every gameweek, call the final table, and see who actually knows football.

How it works

🎯
Predict Gameweek — enter a scoreline for every match before kickoff. Once a match starts, that prediction locks. Predict high/low scoring teams/games to gain extra points
📋
Predict League Table — Set your predicted final standings before transfer window closes. You can change it during second transfer window again.
🗂️
Table — compare everyone's table predictions side by side against the real current standings.

Games — see everyone's scoreline predictions for every gameweek, with crowd sentiment and results.
🏆
Leaderboard — everyone's total points, updated automatically about once an hour.

Season Awards — bragging-rights picks for Golden Boot, Golden Glove, and more.
👤
My Profile — your full prediction history, accuracy, and current streak.
